FAQ answers (collapsed items)

How long does charging take? 20–80% at a DC fast charger in 30 minutes (Standard) or 35 minutes (Extended). 0–100% on a 6.6 kW Level 2 charger in 2.8 h or 3.8 h.

What does the warranty cover? 2 years on the bike and 5 years or 60,000 miles on the battery. The extended warranty adds 2 more years on the bike.

Can I ride one before I buy? Yes. Book a test ride at our showroom, 4100 Thunderbird Road, San Francisco.

Can I finance Ukko? Yes. Fixed-rate loans over 36, 48 or 60 months at 6.99–8.99% APR with 10% down, and a decision within 1 business day.
