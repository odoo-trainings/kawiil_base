FAQ answers (collapsed items)

What are the interest rates? Fixed APR: 6.99% over 36 months, 7.99% over 48 months, 8.99% over 60 months.

How much can I borrow? From $5,000 to $30,000, with a minimum 10% down payment.

Can I pay the loan off early? Yes, with no penalty.

Can I include gear or a service plan? Yes. Add them when you choose your bike, and they're included in the quotation you apply with.
